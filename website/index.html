What is real in this preview?

The navigation, page layouts, FAQ controls, share/copy fallbacks and setup-choice demonstration work. This is not the live SplitHaus product and does not create a house, account or reservation.

  • Price, trial duration, inclusion terms, billing behavior and participation permissions remain unresolved. They are deliberately not invented here.
  • The schedule is a fictional visual explanation, not tested allocation output. Scheduling examples and Match behavior need product verification before publication.
  • The founder quote comes from the reviewed site. The family photo is omitted pending consent.
  • Sharing sends this preview’s URL only at your explicit request. It does not publish the site or track the recipient. Do not send it as an approved offer.
  • This static preview contains no analytics, cookies, local storage or data submission. Public production URLs and instrumentation remain separate implementation work.

  • Trial duration and when it starts.
  • Annual amount, billing unit and what is included.

A few details before you start

Will I be charged when the free trial ends?
Trial and checkout behavior need verification before we can give a definitive answer. This prototype does not collect payment details or create a trial.
What if we let the free trial end?
Read-only access, booking restrictions and calendar continuity after trial expiry need production verification. No preservation or ongoing-access promise is made in this preview.
Does the paid subscription renew automatically?
The authoritative renewal and cancellation terms need confirmation before publication. The live site’s wording alone is not proof of production billing behavior.
What if we share more than one house?
The final offer must state the billing unit and multi-house treatment explicitly. The family’s internal payment arrangement is separate from how the service bills.
