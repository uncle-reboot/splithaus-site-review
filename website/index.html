What's real in this prototype?

  • Navigation, the sample calendar, pricing page, and the setup-to-proposal flow are all genuinely interactive and run only in this tab's memory. Reloading clears everything.
  • There is no account creation, no real save, no data transmission, no payment processing and no email/address collection anywhere in this file.
  • The rotation example uses a simple illustrative round-robin, not the product's real allocator. Draft and Match use your real configured groups and order/shares, but their example outcome calendars use fictional identities and are explicitly marked as examples, not an allocation.
  • Any "Join" action, link or joining-open state is a local simulation only — no real link, revocation or expiry is implemented.
  • Finance figures are fictional sample data for illustration. There is no Pay button and no money ever moves; the tools only record what people say happened elsewhere.
  • The founder quote is carried over from the reviewed public site. No founder photo or testimonial beyond that quote is used.

A few useful questions

Before you decide.

Will it work for how we share?

Explore taking turns, choosing dates in a draft, using Match or booking as you go. You can also assign a starting schedule yourself. Try the approach closest to yours and check the details that matter to your group.

Does everyone need an account?

People who join can book under their own names and receive their own reminders. You can also hold dates you're allowed to book for someone who doesn't use SplitHaus. Those dates appear under your name; that person won't receive their own reminders.

What does setup involve?

Add your groups, choose how you share time and review the schedule or scheduling rules. You can explore before creating an account. You'll need an account to save.

A few billing details

Do I need a card to start?

No card is required to start exploring or to begin the scheduling trial. Shared costs has its own separate 90-day trial.

How is it billed?

Each house is billed separately and annually. If your group shares more than one house, each house has its own subscription.

Does Finance move money between us?

No. Pay outside SplitHaus, then record and confirm the payment here. SplitHaus does not move money between members.
